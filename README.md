# Yiu Hong Ko, Ph.D., RSW

I am a cognitive neuroscientist at Hong Kong Metropolitan University. My research focuses on metacognition, decision confidence, error processing, individual differences, and aging. I use electroencephalography (EEG) and perceptual decision-making tasks to investigate the neural and cognitive mechanisms of decision making. I am also a Registered Social Worker (RSW) in Hong Kong. Currently serving as a journal reviewer and welcome invitations from journals, books, and conferences.

Besides research, I also enjoy teaching and creating teaching materials with animation and hands-on examples.
